TITLE
He Fought a War That Had Already Ended

DESCRIPTION
Hiroo Onoda was sent to Lubang, Philippines, in December 1944 and did not stop fighting until March 1974. A two-part true story. Part 2 is next.
Why did he refuse to believe it was over? Part 2 shows who finally ended it.
Wikipedia notes that Filipino reports say Onoda's group killed up to thirty islanders on Lubang; the figure is alleged and unsourced there (covered in Part 2).
