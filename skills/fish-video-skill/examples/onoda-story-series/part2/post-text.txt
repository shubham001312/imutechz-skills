Only His Old Commander Could Stop Him (Part 2)

DESCRIPTION
The ending of Hiroo Onoda's story: how a soldier who kept fighting WWII for nearly 29 years finally came out in March 1974. Part 2 of 2. Watch Part 1 first: [LINK PART 1 HERE]
